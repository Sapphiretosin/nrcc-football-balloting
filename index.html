Authorized administrators can update the score, match status and timeline. Updates sync live to everyone viewing this page.

Undo restores the previous score, match status and minute. Goal events published through the event form also create an undo point.

Publish a match event

Selecting a Team APJ or Team PFO goal here automatically adds one goal to that team's score. You don't need to update that goal manually as well.

🔁 Make a substitution

Choose the team, player coming off, player coming on, and match minute. Publishing will add a substitution card to the live match timeline.

🏅 Publish individual awards

Publish award winners for the match. Each award category keeps its latest winner and appears on the public page in real time.

📝 Edit team squads

Update player names directly here. Enter one player per line, in order: the first 11 are starters and the final 4 are substitutes. Keep 15 names for each team. Changes are saved to Firebase and appear for everyone viewing the website.
